Price a whole bill of materials

Upload the BOM as Excel, CSV or PDF. The agent finds the part number and quantity columns, prices every line across all suppliers at once, and gives you the table back as Excel.

Drop a BOM, or click to choose .xlsx, .csv or .pdf. Title rows, designator columns and repeated part numbers are all handled.

Prices resolve to the quantity in the file. Lines with no quantity are priced at 1, and you can change any of them before running.
